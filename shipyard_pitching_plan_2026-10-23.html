1. The Rules We're Playing By

DAILY MAX (hard cap, any circumstance)
95 pitches
No pitcher exceeds 95 in a calendar day. Period.
FINISH-THE-BATTER
May finish the current batter
Crossing a threshold mid-batter does not force a higher rest tier — but the 95 daily cap still holds.

3. Friday — Game 1

Game 1 · Friday
Plan A · 100 pitches across 4 arms
Marsh 0–35 → Colton 36–65 → Eli 66–85 → Aymes 86–100

Nothing here is about winning Friday at max effort. It's about getting through seven innings while keeping all four of our best arms legally available for Sunday.

If it goes poorly

SituationMoveCost
Marsh laboring — 25+ pitches in an inning, or 3 runs through 2 Pull him at ≤35, bring Colton immediately. None. Marsh still Sunday-legal.
Colton gets hit — 20+ in his first inning Pull at 30, use Aymes for 2 innings. None. Both still Sunday-legal.

Hard stop: if any Friday arm reaches 36, he is done for the weekend. The 35 ceiling is the whole point of Friday.

9. Before First Pitch — Confirm With the Tournament

  • Tournament daily cap. Assumed 95, matching Pitch Smart 13–14. Some events use 85 or a per-day team total.
  • Game length. Plan assumes 7 innings. If 6, every budget gets 15 pitches of slack.
  • Re-entry rule. Can a pitcher return to the mound in the same day after being removed? Plan assumes no.
  • Weekly/event total. Pitch Smart doesn't cap a tournament total; some events do.
  • Saturday game times. Back-to-back games change warm-up and who's available in relief.
