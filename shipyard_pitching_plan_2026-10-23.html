Virginia Bears 13U · Tournament Pitching Plan · V2

Shipyard — October 23–25

4 games total: Friday 1 · Saturday 2 · Sunday 1 (one game Sunday — win and it's over). Built to MLB Pitch Smart 13–14 limits and the tournament's 95-pitch daily cap.
V2 changes (Oct 20): Sunday is a single game, not a doubleheader. The old Game 5 is gone. With one fewer game, Saturday stops being a savings account — all four top arms now get their second outing locked in before Sunday. Friday's budgets were cut (35/30/15/20 instead of 35/30/20/15) and Saturday's workload redistributed so Eli picks up a short Saturday bridge instead of being held for a Sunday start that no longer exists.

1. The Rules We're Playing By

DAILY MAX (hard cap, any circumstance)
95 pitches
No pitcher exceeds 95 in a calendar day. Period.
FINISH-THE-BATTER
May finish the current batter
Crossing a threshold mid-batter does not force a higher rest tier — but the 95 daily cap still holds.

Friday is not about winning at max effort. It's about getting through seven innings while keeping Marsh and Colton legally available for Sunday, and Eli and Aymes on zero-day rest.

5. Sunday — Game 4 (the only Sunday game)

Game 4 · Sunday
Plan A · Marsh start, Colton bridge, Jase close
Marsh 0–65 → Colton 66–90 → Jase 91–100

Marsh threw 35 Friday = one day of rest (Saturday). He's legal and this is the game we protected him for. Colton threw 30 Friday, also legal. Jase threw 15 Saturday — zero-day rest, legal. Eli and Aymes are the emergency arms.

If it goes poorly

SituationMoveCost
Marsh hit hard early (2+ runs in first, 30+ pitches) Colton in immediately — he has a full 65 available. None. Nothing left to save.
Marsh fine but at 65 with a lead Colton to the 6th, then Jase for the last 3 outs. Preserves Marsh's arm for next season, not next game.
Marsh at 65 and we're trailing Go to Colton anyway. Do not chase 95 in a lost game. Arm protection. Season's over either way.

Extra innings Jase to 20, then Eli, then Aymes. Three live arms. This is the last game of the weekend.
Sunday is single elimination. There is no tomorrow to save anyone for. If the game is on the line, the best available arm goes — the only hard line left is the 95 daily cap.

8. Before First Pitch — Confirm With the Tournament

  • Tournament daily cap. Assumed 95, matching Pitch Smart 13–14. Some events use 85 or a per-day team total.
  • Game length. Plan assumes 7 innings. If 6, every budget gets 15 pitches of slack.
  • Re-entry rule. Can a pitcher return to the mound in the same day after being removed? Plan assumes no.
  • Weekly/event total. Pitch Smart doesn't cap a tournament total; some events do.
  • Saturday game times. Back-to-back games change warm-up and who's available in relief.
  • Sunday format. Confirmed single game — if a second Sunday game appears, this plan needs a re-cut.
